Meridrift uploader

A personal desktop tool maintained by Tim Vermeulen for his own YouTube channels. It retrieves video inventories and channel analytics for a local dashboard. A separate uploader client is used to upload videos to the owner's channels.

The metrics dashboard requests read-only access to video details and YouTube Analytics, including private and unlisted videos, views, watch time and subscriber changes. It cannot upload, edit or delete videos.

This tool is for the owner's personal use. It does not offer public registration or access to channel data.
